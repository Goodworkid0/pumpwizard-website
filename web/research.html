What the lab does

A repeatable loop for testing strategy ideas

Each instance observes the same public market environment independently, with its own rules, ledger, and event history.

01

Observe market conditions

We collect market-cap and price observations over time, alongside token and lifecycle context available in the public feed.

02

Apply a defined strategy

Rules govern entry bands, observation windows, position limits, exits, and modeled costs. Configurations remain inspectable.

03

Record forward evidence

Entries, exits, and scheduled outcome windows are preserved so later results can be compared with the original decision.

Constraints and scope

What the numbers mean—and what they do not.

We make the limits visible because a simulation is only useful when its assumptions and missing evidence are clear.

Modeled execution

Fills, costs, and position marks are simulations. A displayed fill is not an on-chain order or evidence that a live trade could have been filled at the same price.

Forward, not backfilled

Once an instance starts, its ledger records what happens next. New variants receive separate instances rather than rewriting an older strategy’s history.

Coverage is reported

Late and missed outcome observations are shown separately. They do not count as on-time evidence, and incomplete coverage is never hidden as a successful result.

No investment output

The lab does not manage funds, provide personal advice, or guarantee a strategy’s future behavior. Results are research artifacts, not trade signals.

Execution conditions we model

  • Bonding-curve or indexed AMM reserve quotes
  • Configured swap fees, fixed network costs, and entry rent
  • Adverse fill allowance and round-trip cost limits
  • Entry / exit latency, expiring signals, and fresh-quote checks
  • Modeled failed attempts that can consume a fee without opening a position
  • Quote gaps and unavailable exits that remain visible in the ledger
We do not recreate Solana mempool ordering, validator behavior, priority-fee auctions, routing, or every live liquidity condition. Parameters are published with each agent configuration.

Forward measurement

Scheduled observations test how outcomes develop after a modeled event. On-time coverage allows a delay of 10% of the horizon, with a minimum of 60 seconds and maximum of one hour.
