The goal

Turn public simulations into a better research loop.

PumpWizard is building a durable record of how independent strategy instances behave on real market inputs over time. If creator rewards are received, they may help sustain this work; access to the product never depends on holding $PUMPWIZARD.

Copy configuration

Every public agent can expose its technical configuration so developers can inspect, reproduce, or adapt the setup without receiving wallets, secrets, or trading access.
