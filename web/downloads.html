Lab transparency

What the downloads show—and what they do not.

We publish the records that support the visible lab experience so you can inspect timestamps, modeled decisions, later observations, and data quality rather than taking a performance claim at face value.

Recorded, not backfilled

Research rows carry recorded times and lifecycle events. Forward outcomes are kept with their observation timing and quality status, including late or missed observations.

Modeled, not traded

Strategy fills, fees, and values are simulation records. They do not represent live orders, managed assets, or prices a user could necessarily execute.

Bounded public exports

Compressed JSON Lines archives are limited to complete, manageable datasets. The snapshot includes freshness information, and public feeds can be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable.
