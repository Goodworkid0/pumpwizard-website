Legal & methodology

Public market research, not a trading service.

PumpWizard monitors publicly available Solana market information, including Pump.fun market activity and supplementary indexed market data. It runs isolated strategy experiments to collect comparable forward data across signals, market-cap ranges, and holding horizons.

What we do

Track and test

We observe fresh market quotes, reserve-implied prices, market-cap estimates, and selected public activity signals. Strategies use those observations to create simulated entries, exits, and wallet marks under defined rules.

Why we do it

Build an evidence base

Each experiment uses a separate virtual balance and immutable configuration. The purpose is to compare strategy behaviour over time, identify failure modes, and publish the resulting telemetry.

What this is not

No investment product

This site does not accept deposits, manage user assets, execute real orders, or offer an opportunity to invest in a bot, strategy, fund, or token.

How the experiments work

Recorded inputs. Modeled execution.

  1. Observe. The service follows public Pump.fun market data and available supporting market quotes.
  2. Screen. Each strategy applies its own market-cap band, observation window, trend or recovery condition, and risk limits.
  3. Model. A qualifying signal is recorded as a delayed simulated fill with modeled fees, adverse execution, and failed-attempt stress.
  4. Measure. Positions are marked only from usable later quotes; strategy P&L, trade history, and configuration remain public.

Data limitations

Market data can be incomplete

Public feeds, RPC endpoints, and indexed quotes can be delayed, missing, stale, inconsistent, or unavailable. Simulated prices and marks are not executable quotes.

No advice or guarantee

Results are not predictions

Historical or simulated outcomes do not predict future results. Nothing on this site is financial, legal, tax, or investment advice, and no profitability or return is promised.

Community token status

Separate from experiments

If a community token or treasury is ever announced, it will be disclosed separately. Token purchases do not fund strategy balances, confer bot ownership, or create a right to returns.

By using this public site, you acknowledge that it is an informational strategy-research dashboard. Do your own research and seek qualified advice for decisions affecting you.
